01 — 关于我

01 — About me

教育者，
也是课程领导者。An educator,
and a curriculum leader.

好的课程领导不是发号施令，而是把理念翻译成课堂里真实发生的学习。 Good curriculum leadership is not about giving orders — it is about translating ideas into learning that genuinely happens in the classroom.

我的工作横跨两条主线：一条是学校层面的课程与教学管理——统筹多套国际课程的目标对齐、师资发展与质量保障；另一条是 IB 课程的专业实践——从学校认证、五年评审，到全球统考阅卷与教师培训。 My work spans two threads: school-level curriculum and teaching management — aligning goals across multiple international programmes, developing faculty, and assuring quality; and professional IB practice — from school authorisation and five-year reviews to global examination marking and teacher training.

这种"管理者 + 一线教育者"的双重视角，让我既能设计制度，也理解制度落地时的课堂细节。我毕业于新加坡国立大学与滑铁卢大学，数学教育的专业训练始终是我的底层语言。 This dual perspective as "leader + classroom educator" lets me both design systems and understand the classroom details when they are put into practice. A graduate of the National University of Singapore and the University of Waterloo, mathematics education remains my foundational language.
